---
name: Opportunity source trust
description: Product rules for listing opportunities and describing their freshness.
---

- Never seed or display fabricated opportunity records or chatbot answers.
- Public opportunity listings must be source-backed and reviewed; users should be able to open the official application page and see a verification timestamp.
- Do not claim real-time refresh, a scheduled ingestion cadence, or production readiness unless it has been verified.
- The 24-hour stale indicator is a UI policy, not proof of any ingestion schedule or freshness guarantee.

**Why:** The user requires source-backed information and does not want unverified freshness or readiness claims.

**How to apply:** Preserve these rules across search, saved listings, ingestion, recommendations, AI responses, and operational status messaging.
